1 Overview & Portal Access

The BANF EC Admin Portal provides two separate finance modules for managing organization expenses:

Procurement

For pre-approved purchases — submit requests before spending, get tiered approval, then upload receipts after purchase. Built directly into the Admin Portal.

Reimbursement

For after-the-fact reimbursements — upload receipts for expenses already paid with personal funds. Features AI-powered receipt scanning with 7 free vision providers. Runs as a dedicated sub-portal embedded via iframe.

IMPORTANT — Procurement-First Rule:
Regular EC members MUST have an approved procurement ticket before submitting a reimbursement. No reimbursement is allowed without an approved PROC-XXXX ticket.

Only the Treasurer, Vice President, and President can submit manual reimbursements without procurement — a warning notification will be triggered when they do so.
Prior-EC Events Excluded: Saraswati Puja 2026 and Sri Lankan Rental belong to the previous EC term and are excluded from current reimbursement approval. All item categorizations are model-suggested — the Treasurer should verify and re-label as needed.

How to Access the Admin Portal

1
Open the Admin Portal

Navigate to banfjax-hash.github.io/banf/v2/admin-portal.html or click "Admin Portal" from the main BANF website.

2
Log In with EC Credentials

Enter your registered email and password. If this is your first time, complete the signup flow. Use "Forgot Password" if needed — the system now persists reset credentials to the backend.

3
You're In!

After login, you'll see the Dashboard. Finance panels are in the sidebar under Finance.

3 Procurement — Submit a New Request

Use procurement when you need to pre-approve a purchase before buying. This ensures budget oversight and proper authorization.

Step-by-Step: Creating a Procurement Request

1
Navigate to Procurement

Click "Procurement" in the sidebar under Finance. The panel shows 4 sections: New Request, Approval Queue, My Requests, and Upload Receipt.

2
Select a Category

In the "New Procurement Request" card, choose a category from the dropdown. Available categories:

Venue / RentalFacility bookings, equipment rental, chairs/tables

3
Enter Amount

Type the estimated dollar amount. This determines the approval tier (see below).

4
Write a Description

Describe what you're buying, the vendor/store, and why it's needed. Be specific — approvers need context to make a decision.

5
Vendor / Store

Enter the vendor name (e.g., "Amazon", "Costco", "Party City"). This helps with tracking and budgeting.

6
Event / Purpose

Specify which event this purchase is for (e.g., "Bosonto Utsob 2026") or the general purpose (e.g., "Office Supplies").

7
Mark as Urgent (Optional)

Check the "Urgent" box if the purchase needs expedited approval. Urgent requests are highlighted for approvers.

8
Click "Submit Request"

The system validates your entries (description required, amount must be greater than $0) and submits to the backend. You'll see a success message with your request ID (e.g., PROC-0001).

How to Approve or Reject (Approvers Only)

1
Go to the Approval Queue

In the Procurement panel, scroll to the "Approval Queue" card. Pending requests appear here with tier badges.

2
Review the Request

Check the category, amount, description, vendor, event, and requester name. Urgent requests have a highlighted badge.

3
Click "Approve" or "Reject"

Approve: Confirms the purchase is authorized. The requester can proceed with buying.
Reject: You'll be prompted to enter a reason. The requester will see the rejection reason.

Important: Only the designated approvers for each tier can approve/reject requests. The system validates your role before processing.

5 Procurement — Upload Receipt

After making an approved purchase, you must upload the receipt to complete the procurement cycle.

1
Find the "Upload Receipt" Card

In the Procurement panel, scroll down to the "Upload Receipt" section.

2
Enter the Request ID

Type your procurement request ID (e.g., PROC-0001). This was provided when you originally submitted the request.

3
Enter the Actual Amount

Type the actual amount paid on the receipt. This may differ from the originally requested amount.

4
Add Receipt Notes (Optional)

Add any notes about the purchase — e.g., if you got a discount, bought from a different vendor, or need to explain a price difference.

5
Click "Submit Receipt"

The system processes the receipt and checks for variance.

Variance Detection: If the actual amount differs from the requested amount by more than 10%, the request moves to "Variance Review" instead of "Payment Pending". The Treasurer will need to review and approve the discrepancy.

Example: Requested $100, actual $115 → 15% variance → flagged for review.

7 Reimbursement — Access the Portal

The Reimbursement module is an AI-powered sub-portal embedded in the Admin Portal. It provides receipt scanning using free AI vision models.

Role Restriction: Only Treasurer, Vice President, and President can access the Reimbursement module. Other EC members will see a restricted-access notice.

Procurement-First Rule: Even within the Reimbursement portal, non-privileged EC members must provide an approved procurement ticket ID (PROC-XXXX). Treasurer, VP, and President can submit without one, but a warning notification is triggered.

Click "Reimbursement" in Sidebar

Wait for Portal to Load

A loading overlay appears briefly while the sub-portal initializes. Your name, email, and role are passed automatically.

3
Optional: Open in Fullscreen

Click the fullscreen button in the panel header to open the reimbursement portal in a new browser tab for a larger view.

Step-by-Step: Creating a Reimbursement Request

Treasurer Re-Label: All receipt item categorizations are model-suggested. The Treasurer can re-label any receipt item with a note stating exactly what the item is for. This ensures accurate financial records.
1
Go to "New Request"

Click "New Request" in the sub-portal sidebar (or click "New Reimbursement" on the Dashboard). Your name and email are auto-filled.

2
Select the Event

Choose which event this expense is for from the "Select Event" dropdown. Options include all BANF events (Bosonto 2026, Durga Puja 2026, etc.) plus general categories like "General Operations", "Website & Technology", and "Other".

3
Upload Receipt(s)

Drag & drop receipt images/PDFs into the drop zone, or click to browse. Supports JPG, PNG, and PDF. You can upload multiple receipts for one request.

4
AI Scans Your Receipt Automatically

The system sends your receipt image to the configured AI vision model. It extracts: store name, date, line items with costs, and total amount. This usually takes 5–15 seconds.

5
Review & Edit Extracted Data

Each receipt gets a card showing extracted fields. Review carefully — you can edit the store name, date, line items, and total if the AI got anything wrong. Click "Add Line Item" to add missing items.

6
Confirm Each Receipt

Click the "Confirm Receipt" button on each card. The button turns green when confirmed. You must confirm all receipts before submitting.

7
Fill Payment Details

In the "Payment & Submission" card:

FieldRequired?Options / Details
Procurement Ticket IDYes*PROC-XXXX — your approved procurement ticket. Required for regular EC members. Optional for Treasurer/VP/President (manual reimbursement triggers warning).
Paid ByYesPersonal Funds (Reimbursement), BANF Card, Cash, Other
Budget Pre-Approved ByNoPresident, Treasurer, Vice President, or leave blank
Additional NotesNoContext for the Treasurer
8
Click "Submit Reimbursement Request"

The system validates: event selected, name/email filled, at least 1 receipt uploaded, and all receipts confirmed. On success, you'll see a confirmation message and the pipeline progresses to the "Submit" step.

Missing Receipt? Click "Add Missing Receipt" to create a receipt entry without an image. You'll need to provide a reason explaining why the receipt is missing.

12 FAQ & Troubleshooting

What's the difference between Procurement and Reimbursement?

Procurement = Get approval BEFORE spending. Submit a request → get approved → make the purchase → upload receipt.
Reimbursement = Already spent your own money. Upload receipt(s) → AI scans them → submit for review → get reimbursed.

I can't access the Reimbursement panel?

Only the Treasurer, Vice President, and President can access the Reimbursement panel directly. Other EC members must:

  1. Go to Procurement and submit a request
  2. Wait for approval (PROC-XXXX ticket must be approved)
  3. Contact the Treasurer with the approved procurement ticket ID for reimbursement processing

The Treasurer, VP, or President can process the reimbursement on your behalf using the approved ticket reference.

What about Saraswati Puja 2026 and Sri Lankan Rental expenses?

These events belong to the prior EC term and are excluded from current reimbursement approval. If you have pending expenses from these events, contact the Treasurer directly — they require special handling and verification.

The AI categorized my receipt items incorrectly — what happens?

All categorizations are model-suggested. The Treasurer has the ability to re-label any receipt item with a note stating exactly what the item is for. This is part of the normal review process — just submit as-is and the Treasurer will verify.

My receipt scan failed or looks wrong — what do I do?

The AI extraction is not always perfect. You can manually edit every field (store name, date, line items, total). Just correct the values and click "Confirm Receipt".

If AI keeps failing, try switching to a different provider in the AI Engine settings. Google Gemini and Groq have the best success rate.

What happens if my actual purchase amount differs from what I requested?

When you upload a receipt, if the actual amount differs from the requested amount by more than 10%, the request enters "Variance Review" status. The Treasurer must approve the discrepancy before payment can proceed.

I lost my receipt — can I still submit?

In the Reimbursement portal, click "Add Missing Receipt". You'll need to manually enter the store, amount, and explain why the receipt is missing. Missing-receipt requests may face additional scrutiny during approval.

How do I track the status of my request?

Procurement: Check "My Requests & Receipts" in the Procurement panel — color-coded borders show status instantly.
Reimbursement: The Dashboard shows KPIs (submitted, parsed, confirmed) and the processing pipeline shows which step your request is at.

Can I submit both procurement and reimbursement for the same expense?

No — use one or the other. If you got pre-approval via procurement, upload the receipt there. If you paid out-of-pocket without prior approval, use reimbursement.
