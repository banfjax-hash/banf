  1. The vendor is allotted a 10 ft × 6 ft stall area at the Noboborsho 2026 event venue.
  2. Tables and chairs will be provided by BANF. All other setup materials, decorations, and displays are the vendor's responsibility.
  3. The vendor must set up their stall starting at 10:30 AM and complete teardown by 3:00 PM.
  4. The stall fee of $100.00 is non-refundable and covers the stall space for the full event day.
  5. The vendor must comply with all local health and safety regulations applicable to their products or services.
  6. BANF reserves the right to approve the types of goods/services offered at the stall to ensure they align with the event's family-friendly nature.
  7. The vendor will maintain cleanliness of their stall area and surrounding space during and after the event.
  8. BANF is not liable for any loss, damage, or theft of the vendor's belongings or merchandise during the event.
