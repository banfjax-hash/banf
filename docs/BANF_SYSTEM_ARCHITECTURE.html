§0  Problem Statement & Vision ▼

🏠 About BANF

The Bengali Association of North Florida (BANF) is a cultural nonprofit community organization based in Jacksonville, FL. It serves 180+ member families of Bengali heritage, organizing 17+ events per year — from Durga Puja and Saraswati Puja to Bosonto Utsob (Noboborsho), Rabindra-Nazrul Jayanti, picnics, and more.

⚠️ The Problem

📊 Scattered Data

Member records spread across Google Sheets, personal email, WhatsApp groups, and handwritten notes. No single source of truth.

🔄 Annual EC Turnover

Executive Committee changes every year. Institutional memory is lost with each transition — passwords, contacts, vendor relationships, event history all walk out the door.

💸 Manual Finance Tracking

Payment tracking via screenshots, WhatsApp confirmations, and manual spreadsheets. No reconciliation between Zelle receipts, bank statements, and member records.

📧 Communication Chaos

RSVP tracked via Evite (unreliable), email blasts from personal Gmail, no automated follow-ups, no delivery tracking, no response classification.

🎪 Event Management Overload

17+ events/year — each requiring venue booking, RSVP management, food counts, volunteer coordination, expense tracking. All done manually by volunteers.

🤷 No Self-Service

Members can't look up their own payment history, RSVP status, or membership tier. Every query goes to EC members who are volunteers themselves.

🚀 The Vision

Build an AI-powered, fully automated community management platform that eliminates manual overhead and preserves institutional memory across EC transitions.

Vision: From Manual to Autonomous
❌ Before
Spreadsheets, WhatsApp, manual email, lost history, scattered payments
⟹
✅ After
Unified platform, AI agents, auto-reconciliation, persistent memory, self-service portals

Key Design Principles

  • Zero Manual Overhead — Every recurring task is automated via agents and pipelines
  • Institutional Memory — All decisions, communications, and financials are permanently recorded in 65 data collections
  • EC-Transition Resilient — New EC members onboard via guided flows; historical context is preserved
  • AI-First — Member queries answered by RAG-powered chatbot; emails auto-classified and routed; payments auto-reconciled
  • Self-Service — Members, EC, and admins each get purpose-built portals requiring zero training
