Executive Summary

The BANF Digital Platform is a comprehensive community management system designed to serve the Bengali Association of North Florida, consolidating member management, event coordination, financial operations, and communication into a unified system with intelligent automation capabilities.

Key Capabilities

Multi-tier Membership

7 membership categories with 4 household tiers each, supporting family, couple, individual, and student options.

Role-Based Access Control

Hierarchical RBAC with 40+ permissions mapped to 5 role levels from Guest to Super Admin.

AI-Powered Automation

LLM agents for email triage, auto-response generation, and intelligent member services.

Cross-Platform Delivery

Progressive Web App, native iOS (SwiftUI), and Android (Jetpack Compose) applications.

Business Context

BANF serves approximately 200+ Bengali families in the Jacksonville, Florida area. The organization manages:

  • 10+ annual events including Durga Puja, Saraswati Puja, cultural programs
  • Fiscal year operations from AprilΓÇôMarch
  • 7 membership tiers ranging from $100 to $400+

Major Workflows

EC Onboarding Gate Chain

The EC Onboarding Gate enforces a strict operational sequence at the start of each fiscal year:

EC Onboarding
Super Admin adds EC roles
Members set passwords
Profile setup complete
Membership Drive
Gated until EC complete
President launches drive
Invites sent to members
Member Login
Gated until onboarded
Members register via drive
Pay dues to activate

Membership Drive Lifecycle

1 READY: Gate check passes, configuration loaded
2 LAUNCHED: President activates drive, invites sent to prior-year members
3 ACTIVE: Members register, select tier, make payments
4 CLOSED: Deadline passed, late registration handling
5 COMPLETE: Reports generated, reconciliation finalized
