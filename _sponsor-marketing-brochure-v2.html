Who We Are

Founded in 2008, the Bengali Association of North Florida (BANF) is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit that preserves Bengali culture, fosters community connections, and creates meaningful impact in Jacksonville. Our community comprises professionals, entrepreneurs, healthcare providers, IT specialists, educators, and families who are highly engaged, digitally connected, and actively involved in local commerce and services.

In a historic recognition, the Mayor of Jacksonville officially proclaimed August 9th as Bengali Cultural Heritage Day — a testament to our community's significance. We partner with the Jacksonville City Council on charity initiatives including community beach cleanup drives, demonstrating our commitment to civic responsibility.
